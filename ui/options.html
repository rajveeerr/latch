      Help

      Lock from the keyboard. ⌃⇧L locks this profile.

      Forgot your passcode? On the lock screen choose Forgot passcode and enter your recovery code. Without it, remove Latch at chrome://extensions and add it again. Your tabs are untouched.

      Something stuck? Turn Latch off in chrome://extensions. Covers disappear at once. If a tab is still muted or a window minimized, unmute it or restore it from the Dock.
