Latch

Lock this browser profile behind a passcode.

Passcode

Choose a passcode. Longer is safer; a short numeric code can be guessed offline by someone who copies your profile folder.

When to lock

What locking does

Latch never closes, reloads or edits your tabs, tab groups or windows.

Protection

Change the shortcut at brave://extensions/shortcuts (default: Control+Shift+L). To remove Latch entirely, use brave://extensions; your tabs are untouched either way.
